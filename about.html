Our Story

Founded with a passion for authentic Indian street food, Payal Chat began as a small dream to bring the vibrant flavors of the streets to your plate. We believe that chat is not just food; it's an explosion of culture, tanginess, and joy.

From our signature Chhole Bhature to the crisp perfection of our Golgappas, every dish is prepared using traditional recipes passed down through generations, ensuring that every bite tastes like home.
